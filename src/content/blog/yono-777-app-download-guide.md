## Questions to ask about any 777-named app

Because the label is so common, it helps to have a standard set of questions you can apply to any app that uses it:

- **Who publishes it?** A named publisher you can trace is better than an anonymous one.
- **Is there an official page?** Not just a download page, but a page that describes the app and links to support.
- **What are the terms?** Can you read them without installing the app?
- **What does it ask for?** Compare the permission list with what a slots-style game needs.
- **How old is the listing?** A long, consistent history is a better sign than a page that appeared this week. Our guide to [checking what is really new](/blog/new-yono-games-how-to-verify) shows how.

If you cannot answer most of these, the safest move is to wait.

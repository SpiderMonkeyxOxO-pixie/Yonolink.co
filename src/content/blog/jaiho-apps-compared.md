## What "Shared Wallet" Actually Means Here

It's not a loyalty-points gimmick layered on top of separate accounts — it's one balance. Jaiho Rummy's own listing describes a "cross-platform wallet" that works across Jaiho Slot and Jaiho Spin without a separate top-up. Jaiho Spin's winnings land in that same wallet and are immediately usable at Jaiho Rummy tables. Jaiho Slot goes further: rummy winnings can fund a slot session and the reverse works too. Jaiho Arcade sits on top of all of it as a single lobby for Jaiho Rummy, Jaiho Slot, Jaiho Spin and Jaiho Win, so installing Arcade is functionally the same account as installing any one of the others. Jaiho Win isn't a game at all — it's a dashboard that tracks lifetime winnings, active bonuses and withdrawal status across every Jaiho app you use, with an auto-withdrawal trigger you set yourself.

Jaiho91 is the one with a real distinction worth knowing before you pick it: it's a curated tier of 91 games, each required to hold at least 91% audited RTP, re-checked monthly with underperformers swapped out. An existing Jaiho login works on it immediately, but it's a narrower, higher-bar library than Arcade's full catalogue, not a replacement for it.

Jaiho Arcade, at ₹250.

**Is Jaiho the same company as Yono?**
No. Jaiho apps are a separate family with their own developer, accounts and download links. Some Jaiho apps happen to sit in the same site category ("Arcade") as Yono-branded apps, but that's a classification, not a shared operator.

**Can I use one Jaiho login on all seven apps?**
Yes, for the six with a confirmed shared wallet. Jaiho91 and Jaiho Win also work as standalone registrations if you'd rather not use an existing Jaiho account.

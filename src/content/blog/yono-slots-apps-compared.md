## Before You Assume a Bonus Is Cash

A "confirmed" figure is the amount the app states for new users on a qualifying first deposit. It's a stated number, not necessarily spendable cash the second it lands — some of these platforms run bonus credit through a separate balance that needs a wagering or playthrough requirement cleared first. None of that detail fits in a comparison table; it's on the individual app's page.

Scanning purely for the biggest headline figure puts Yono 777's ₹500 at the top here. The deposit tiers and fine print behind that number, though, only exist on Yono 777's own page.

## Tips for Playing Yono Rummy

**Understand the format first.** Points Rummy, Pool Rummy and Deals Rummy have different structures. Know which format a table is running before joining.

**Start at lower stakes.** Most apps have beginner tables with lower entry fees. Use these to practice before moving to higher-stake tables.

**Prioritise pure sequences.** In standard 13-card Rummy, a pure sequence (without a joker) is mandatory. Focus on completing it early.

**Track your discards.** Pay attention to what cards your opponents pick and discard — it reveals what they're building.

**Use the referral program.** Most Yono Rummy apps offer rewards for referring friends. This is one of the most consistent ways to earn bonus cash without additional deposits.

**Withdraw regularly.** Don't leave large balances sitting in your app wallet. Withdraw winnings to UPI regularly to maintain clear records of your activity.

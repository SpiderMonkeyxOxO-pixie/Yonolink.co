## A Confirmed Figure Isn't the Same as Cash in Hand

"Confirmed" just means the app states this amount for new users on a qualifying first deposit — it doesn't mean the money lands as withdrawable cash the moment you deposit. A number of these platforms route the credit through a separate balance first, with its own wagering or playthrough requirement before it converts. That's app-specific detail this table doesn't carry; the linked app page does.

Yono Games' ₹500 is the category's ceiling if you're just scanning for the biggest headline number. But the full picture — deposit tiers, any strings attached — only lives on the individual app page, not here.

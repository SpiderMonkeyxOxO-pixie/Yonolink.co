## Two Ways In, Same Category

Neta VIP is invitation-gated — you apply, and the platform decides based on identity verification, banking checks, and a minimum activity level. In return, members get a dedicated account manager, custom tournament invitations, and exclusive ₹1 crore monthly events.

Yono VIP is deposit-gated — no application at all. Cross ₹10,000 in cumulative Yono deposits and it unlocks automatically, with 3x loyalty points on every game played, VIP-only tournaments across rummy, slots and 777, and a monthly cashback of up to ₹5,000 on net losses.

Neither path is "better" outright. Neta VIP suits someone who wants white-glove treatment and is willing to go through verification up front. Yono VIP suits someone who's already an active, higher-spending Yono player and would rather unlock status through natural play than apply for it.

## Reading the Bonus Figures

₹500 vs ₹400 looks like a simple gap, but the two bonuses sit inside very different programs — one requires qualifying through verification first, the other requires reaching a deposit threshold first. Neither number should be the only reason to pick one over the other: the ongoing perks (account manager and exclusive events for Neta VIP; loyalty multiplier and cashback for Yono VIP) matter more than the entry bonus once you're actually in.

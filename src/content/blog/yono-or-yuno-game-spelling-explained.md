## What search engines do with misspellings

Search engines are good at guessing what you meant, which is why a search for "yuno game" often shows results for "Yono games". That convenience has a catch. The pages that rank for a misspelling are sometimes the ones that deliberately target the typo, and those are not always the most careful sources. If a page uses the wrong spelling throughout, with no sign that it knows the correct name, treat it as a lower-trust result.

A simple habit helps: after your first search, re-search with the correct spelling and the category you want, such as "Yono rummy" or "Yono arcade". Compare the two result lists. The overlap is usually where the better sources sit.

If you're installing more than one Yono app, or working with limited storage on an older device, app size matters more than it might seem.

---

## General size range across the directory

Individual Yono APKs typically range from around 20MB to 80MB, as covered in our [APK download guide](/blog/how-to-download-yono-game-apk). Category tends to be a rough guide: Arcade apps, which bundle multiple game types, tend to sit toward the larger end of that range, while single-format Rummy or Spin apps are often smaller.

---

## Planning storage for multiple installs

If you're installing several apps from the same category to compare them — several Rummy apps, for example — budget roughly 300–500MB of free storage to be safe, on top of whatever else is already on your device. Installed data (game progress, cached assets) can also grow the footprint slightly beyond the initial APK size over time.

---

## Freeing up space before installing

- Check **Settings → Storage** to see what's actually using space before assuming you need to delete an app entirely.
- Clear cached data for existing apps first — this is usually reversible and frees space without losing your account.
- Uninstall apps you've already decided against, rather than leaving multiple similar Rummy or Slots apps installed "just in case."

---

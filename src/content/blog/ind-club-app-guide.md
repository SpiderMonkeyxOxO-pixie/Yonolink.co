## Understanding a referral-tier model before you join

A referral-tier system is different from a flat welcome bonus in one important way: your benefit depends on other people's actions, not just your own sign-up. Before inviting friends to help you reach Silver or Gold, check:

- Whether referred friends need to deposit, or just register, for the referral to count.
- Whether tier status can be lost if referred friends become inactive.
- Whether "member-only" tournaments have entry conditions beyond reaching the tier.
- Whether the cashback or prize-pool percentage is fixed or can change without notice.

An app that makes these terms hard to find inside the app itself has told you something useful about how carefully you should proceed.

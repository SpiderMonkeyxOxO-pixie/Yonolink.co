## Questions to ask inside the app

Once it is installed, look for answers to these before you do anything else:

- Where are the terms and privacy policy?
- Is there a visible support contact?
- Are there limit or break tools for responsible play?
- What does the app say about identity verification?

An app that makes these hard to find has told you something useful.

## How to compare it before choosing

Rather than picking an app because of a name, compare two or three on identical criteria. Note down, for each one, whether the terms are easy to find, whether support contact details are visible, what permissions the installer requests, which formats are offered, and what the app says about identity checks. Put the answers side by side. The app that is clearest about its rules is usually the better-run one, whatever its name suggests.

It also helps to compare against a slower, more cautious option: waiting a week. A listing that still looks consistent after seven days, with the same terms and the same source, has passed a simple test that many hurried listings fail.

All 56 apps in this directory are free to download, with no upfront purchase price. That's a simple fact — but it's easy to conflate "free to download" with "free to play for real money," which is a different question entirely.

---

## What "free to download" covers

- No purchase price to install the APK
- No subscription or account-creation fee
- No cost to browse the app or create an account

---

## What it doesn't cover

- **Deposits are separate.** Most apps in this category are real-cash gaming platforms — playing for money requires depositing your own funds, which is unrelated to the free download.
- **In-app purchases may exist.** Some apps offer optional paid features beyond standard deposits; check each app's own terms.
- **"Free" isn't a safety signal.** A free download says nothing about an app's legitimacy, payout reliability, or terms — see our [APK safety checklist](/blog/how-to-download-yono-game-apk) for what actually matters before installing.

---

## Why this distinction matters

Marketing language across this app category sometimes blurs "free" (the download) with "free money" (a bonus or reward) in ways that can be misleading if you're not reading carefully. Downloading costs nothing; what you choose to deposit afterward is an entirely separate decision, with its own terms specific to that platform.

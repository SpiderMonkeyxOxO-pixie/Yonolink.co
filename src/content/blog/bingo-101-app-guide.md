## How 90-ball bingo works here

Standard 90-ball bingo uses cards with 27 squares and balls numbered 1–90, paying out at three stages within a single game: 1-line, 2-line, and Full House. Bingo 101's own listing states it layers 101 different winning-pattern variations on top of that base format across a day's sessions, with the day's pattern schedule published 24 hours in advance so players can plan which sessions to enter.

## Auto-daub and multi-card play

The app's stated features include an auto-daub function that marks called numbers automatically across all of a player's active cards, and support for up to 12 bingo cards per game. Auto-daub matters specifically because of that multi-card ceiling — manually marking 12 cards by hand against a fast number-call isn't realistic, so the automation is what makes the higher card counts usable at all. Treat the 12-card limit and the automation behavior as the app's own stated design, something to confirm once the app is actually installed rather than assumed in advance.

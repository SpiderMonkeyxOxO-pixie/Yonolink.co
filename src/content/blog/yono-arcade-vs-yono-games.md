"Yono Arcade" and "Yono Games" are two separate apps that both sit in the Arcade category of this directory — and their similar names make them genuinely easy to confuse in a search result or a forwarded link.

---

## They are two different apps

[Yono Arcade](/app/yono-arcade) and [Yono Games](/app/yono-games) are separate, individually listed platforms in this directory. Both are classified under our [Arcade category](/blog/yono-arcade-category), but a download link, promo code, or account for one does not apply to the other.

---

## How to tell which one you have

- Check the exact app name on the install screen or your device's app drawer — the two names are close but not identical.
- Compare the download source against the specific app page you intended to visit — [Yono Arcade](/app/yono-arcade) or [Yono Games](/app/yono-games) — rather than a generic "Yono Arcade games" search result.
- Don't assume a promo code or bonus described for one app works on the other.

---

## Why the confusion happens

Both apps share the word "Yono" and sit in the same Arcade category, and "arcade" itself is also a generic term used loosely across marketing pages in this category — not just as this specific app's name. That overlap in naming, not any relationship between the two apps, is what drives the mix-up.
